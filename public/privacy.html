Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 11, 2026

1. What we collect

When you create a ProjectForge account we collect your name, email address, and any profile details you choose to add (photo, phone, bio, skills). Projects, tasks, comments, and files you create are stored so the product can function.

2. How we use it

We use your data to operate your account, sync your projects in real time across devices, send notifications you've enabled, and improve the product. We do not sell your personal data.

3. Where it's stored

Data is stored in Firebase Cloud Firestore and Firebase Storage, protected by Firebase Authentication and access rules that scope every project to its actual members.

4. Sharing

Your project data is visible only to the members you invite to that project, and to platform administrators for support and moderation purposes. We may share data with service providers (such as our hosting and email infrastructure) solely to operate the product.

5. Your choices

  • Update or delete your profile information at any time from Settings.
  • Export or delete a project you own from that project's settings.
  • Request full account deletion by contacting us.

6. Cookies & local storage

We use local storage to remember your theme preference and session state. We don't use third-party advertising trackers.

7. Contact

Questions about this policy? Reach us any time at our contact page.
